## About the Platform

 `Mars3D Platform` is a 3D client development platform powered by WebGL. Built on top of [Cesium](https://cesium.com/cesiumjs/) with optimizations and enhancements and designed with a B/S architecture, it is a lightweight, high-performance GIS development platform that supports extension across many industries. It runs efficiently in a browser with no installation and no plug-ins, can quickly integrate and consume a wide variety of GIS data and 3D models, renders 3D spatial visualization, and enables the platform to be applied flexibly in different industries.
